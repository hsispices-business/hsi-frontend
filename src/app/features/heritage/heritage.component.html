26 Years of Himalayan Soul.

From the mist-shrouded peaks of Solan, Himachal Pradesh, Him Spices Industries has been the custodian of authentic Indian flavor for over a quarter-century.

The Solan Standard.

Our story began in the crisp mountain air of Solan, where the terrain itself demands resilience and rewards patience. Since 1998, HSI has operated on a singular principle: that the purity of the source defines the quality of the spice.

We don't just manufacture; we preserve. The high altitude and temperate climate of our base in Himachal Pradesh provide the ideal environment for processing delicate botanicals without losing their essential oils and aromatic volatility.
